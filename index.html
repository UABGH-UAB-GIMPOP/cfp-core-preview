About the national core

The national Community, Family and Peer Support Core supports diabetes translational research that considers the relationships around people, not just individual behavior.

Its work includes research consultation and resources for developing, implementing and evaluating support programs in communities and health care settings.

This proposed site is being developed for the core’s next chapter. Leadership, participating institutions and service details will be confirmed before launch.
